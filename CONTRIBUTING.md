# **Contributing to the Veritas Manifesto**
Thank you for your interest in refining the **Veritas Model** for ethical governance. This project thrives on critical discussion, structural improvements, and real-world applications.  

## **📌 Ways to Contribute**
✔ **Submit Issues** – Identify potential flaws or propose enhancements in the [Issues tab](../../issues).  
✔ **Pull Requests** – If you have a structural refinement, submit a PR with proposed edits to **Veritas_Manifesto.md**.  
✔ **Propose Experiments** – Want to test this model in a small-scale governance setting? Share your insights in [Discussions](../../discussions).  

## **🛠 Guidelines for Contributions**
🔹 Keep refinements **solution-driven**—identify issues, but also propose fixes.  
🔹 Cite **historical or modern governance examples** when suggesting changes.  
🔹 Be specific—structural reforms should be actionable, not just conceptual.  

---

## **💡 Key Areas for Refinement**
🚧 **Governance Rotation Timing** – Should leader rotation intervals be uniform or context-dependent?  
🚧 **AI Auditing Mechanisms** – How do we ensure AI oversight remains neutral over time?  
🚧 **Implementation Feasibility** – What small-scale test cases could validate these ideas?  

Your contributions will help shape **a real-world alternative to corruptible governance.** Let’s build something better.
